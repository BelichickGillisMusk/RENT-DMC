Brief

Solar canopies for California public parking.

AI-tracked canopies over lots agencies, campuses, and fleets already operate. Focus: public jobs. Geography: all of California.

What it is

A tracked solar canopy system for existing parking — shade, on-site generation, and EV-ready layout. Not a field array. Not a passive carport copy-paste.

You get

  • Covered parking people notice
  • Generation aimed at site load
  • Path to L2 / DCFC / NACS / CCS
  • Phased plan if the utility is slow

You control

  • Charger brand and network
  • Phasing (lot A now, lot B later)
  • Prime vs sub / teaming path
  • Go / no-go after fit check

Who this is for

Public jobs first. Private multifamily and retail are secondary.

  • City, county, and state parking & corporation yards
  • Community colleges and university lots
  • Municipal and institutional fleets
  • Military and federal parking (see Military page)

Where

All of California — Bay Area, Sacramento, Central Valley, Central Coast, LA Basin, Inland Empire, San Diego, and public lots in between. Not limited to one metro.

How a pilot works

  1. Share the site Agency name, address, stall count, photos, solicitation # if any.
  2. Fit check Layout, energy story, buy path — practical yes/no.
  3. Concept Canopy approach, charging options, phasing, licensed install scope.
  4. Procure & stage Bid or teaming, design, permits, build around operations.

What to send

  • Agency or ownership name
  • Site address and stall count (or acreage)
  • Charger goals (none / L2 / DCFC / mixed)
  • Timeline or RFP number if live

Yield is site-modeled — not a universal percentage. Licenses and certifications are stated only when active and verifiable.
